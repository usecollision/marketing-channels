# marketing-channels

The **distribute** layer of the UseCollision Marketing OS. Every channel you execute on: search (organic + AI), social, email, and outbound.

Merged from: `marketing-seo` + `marketing-ai-search` + `marketing-social` + `marketing-email` + `marketing-outbound` (Aug 2026).

## Skills

| Skill | Category | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [keyword-research](keyword-research/SKILL.md) | seo | Systematic keyword research for topical authority, content planning, and search visibility. |
| [seo-audit](seo-audit/SKILL.md) | seo | Run a comprehensive 5-pillar SEO audit covering technical, on-page, content, authority, and performance. |
| [ai-search-audit](ai-search-audit/SKILL.md) | ai-search | Audit your brand visibility across AI search surfaces — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot. |
| [linkedin-content](linkedin-content/SKILL.md) | social | Build LinkedIn personal brand and company presence with high-engagement content frameworks. |
| [social-strategy](social-strategy/SKILL.md) | social | Build a cross-platform social media strategy aligned to growth goals and ICP presence. |
| [lifecycle-sequences](lifecycle-sequences/SKILL.md) | email | Design complete lifecycle email programs covering onboarding, activation, retention, and reactivation. |
| [cold-email-sequence](cold-email-sequence/SKILL.md) | outbound | Write high-converting B2B cold email sequences with research-driven personalization and follow-up structure. |
