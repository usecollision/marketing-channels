## When to Use

Invoke when:
- Building a LinkedIn presence from scratch
- Want to grow audience for lead gen or brand building
- Need to write specific LinkedIn posts
- Planning a LinkedIn content calendar
- Engagement is low on existing posts

## Workflow

### Step 1: Profile & Positioning
Before posting, optimize the foundation:
- Headline: Not job title but value statement (what you help people do)
- About: Story-driven, pain → solution → proof → CTA
- Featured: Top content, lead magnets, case studies
- Banner: Clear value prop or social proof

**Gate:** Profile clearly communicates who you help and what you're known for.

### Step 2: Content Pillars (3-5)
Choose pillars that balance:
- Authority building (expertise in your domain)
- Relatability (behind-the-scenes, failures, learnings)
- Engagement (hot takes, questions, polls)
- Conversion (case studies, results, offers)

Formula: 40% value/education + 30% story/relatability + 20% engagement + 10% promotion

**Gate:** 3-5 pillars defined with example topics for each.

### Step 3: Post Frameworks
Use proven LinkedIn post structures:

**Hook + Story + Lesson:**
- Hook: 1 line that stops scroll (contrarian, specific result, question)
- Story: 3-5 short paragraphs telling what happened
- Lesson: Key takeaway formatted as list or one-liner
- CTA: Question or invitation to engage

**List Post:**
- Hook: "X things I learned about [topic]"
- List: 5-10 items, each 1-2 lines
- Closer: Summary insight + CTA

**Contrarian/Hot Take:**
- Hook: "Unpopular opinion: [statement]"
- Reasoning: 3-4 supporting points
- Nuance: Acknowledge the other side
- CTA: "Agree or disagree?"

**Before/After:**
- Before: "6 months ago I was [struggling with X]"
- Turning point: What changed
- After: Results and current state
- Lesson: What others can take from this

**Gate:** 4+ post drafts using different frameworks.

### Step 4: Writing Rules
Apply to every post:
- First line is everything (hook must stop the scroll)
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences max)
- White space between every line
- Use line breaks aggressively
- Write at 8th grade level (clear > clever)
- End with engagement hook (question, opinion request)
- No external links in post body (kills reach; put in comments)
- Post length: 800-1300 characters for optimal reach
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags max

**Gate:** Posts follow formatting rules and pass the "would I stop scrolling for this?" test.

### Step 5: Engagement Strategy
Posting is 50% of LinkedIn growth. The other 50%:
- Comment on 10-15 posts from target audience daily (before and after posting)
- Reply to every comment on your posts within 2 hours
- DM new connections with genuine value (not pitch)
- Engage with competitor/peer content thoughtfully
- Post consistently (3-5x/week minimum for growth)

Best posting times: Tue-Thu, 7-9am local time of your audience.

**Gate:** Engagement routine defined and sustainable for your schedule.

## Evaluation & QA

### Common Failure Modes
- All promotion, no value (nobody follows an ad account)
- Inconsistent posting (algorithm rewards consistency)
- No hook (first line decides if post gets read)
- External links in body (LinkedIn suppresses reach)
- Not engaging with others (social is a two-way street)
- Too polished/corporate (authenticity wins on LinkedIn)
