## When to Use

Invoke when:
- Starting short-form video for a brand or founder
- Reels/TikToks get views but no followers
- Videos die in the first second (low retention)
- Setting a realistic posting cadence
- Expanding from one platform to the other

## Workflow

### Step 1: Platform & Algorithm Primer
- **Shared core** - both feed rankings reward watch time, completion rate, saves, shares, and rewatches; follower count matters less than per-video quality signals
- **Instagram Reels** - favors polished, aesthetic, on-brand content; saves and sends-to-friends are strong signals; works with 2-3 posts per week
- **TikTok** - favors raw, fast, trend-aware content; higher posting volume is common practice (heuristic - daily posting is widespread); sounds and trends matter more
- **Algorithm notes** - platform details change often; verify current ranking factors before building the strategy (check official creator documentation)

**Gate:** Written platform notes with current algorithm signals, dated and sourced.

### Step 2: Format Mastery
- **Specs** - 9:16 vertical, safe zones for UI overlays, captions burned in (most viewers watch muted - widely reported pattern, verify on your own retention data)
- **Length** - as short as the idea allows; heuristics - hook in the first 2 seconds, the first 3 seconds decide retention
- **Structure** - hook → context → payoff → loop; end on a line that loops back to the start for rewatches
- **Series formats** - recurring formats ("3 mistakes", "how I X in 60 seconds") that viewers learn to recognize
- **Posting surface** - Reels feed the Instagram Reels tab and can also go to the grid; TikTok videos cross-post to YouTube Shorts where capacity allows

**Gate:** Format playbook with specs, structure template, and 2-3 series formats defined.

### Step 3: Hook Bank
Write hooks before scripts:

- **Patterns** - contrarian claim, mistake reveal, number-led list, POV role-play, "stop doing X", curiosity gap, before/after
- **First line = caption line** - the spoken hook and the caption hook should match
- **Pattern interrupt** - movement, prop, or visual change in the first second
- **Hook bank discipline** - log every hook with its retention data; reuse only proven patterns
- **No bait-and-switch** - the hook must be satisfied by the video or retention collapses

**Gate:** Bank of 20+ written hooks tagged by pattern, ready for production.

### Step 4: Content Engine & Cadence
- **Cadence** - pick a sustainable floor (heuristics - 3+ Reels/week for Instagram, 5-7/week for TikTok; consistency beats bursts); batch production days
- **Production workflow** - idea backlog → hook-first scripts → batch filming → edit with templates → caption/publish checklist
- **Repurposing pipeline** - long-form (webinars, podcasts, YouTube) cut into clips; clips ladder up into long-form (see youtube-strategy)
- **Trend policy** - jump only on trends that fit the niche, within 24-48 hours of the trend peaking (heuristic window); skip everything else
- **Creator comfort** - talking-head vs voiceover vs b-roll formats matched to the founder's comfort

**Gate:** Production calendar with batch days, repurposing pipeline, and trend policy.

### Step 5: Growth Loops & Community Engagement
- **Comment loop** - reply to every early comment; pin a question comment to seed the section
- **Reply with video** - turn good comments into follow-up videos (a proven organic loop on both platforms)
- **Stitches/duets/remixes** - engage adjacent creators' content with added value, not clout-chasing
- **Cross-platform seeding** - share Reels to X/LinkedIn where the audience overlaps (see x-twitter-growth)
- **Funnel to owned channels** - bio link → newsletter signup or lead magnet; TikTok → Instagram → email is a common chain (see newsletter-operations)
- **Engagement hygiene** - no follow-for-follow or bot engagement; platforms detect and suppress it

**Gate:** Engagement routine defined with daily reply blocks and a bio-link funnel.

### Step 6: Analytics & Iteration
- **Core metrics** - views, average watch time, completion rate, retention graph, saves/shares, profile visits, follower conversion
- **Retention first** - the retention curve shows where the video loses people; fix the weakest point first (heuristic - a cliff at second 1 means hook failure)
- **Top-decile review** - weekly, compare your top videos with your median; extract the pattern and repeat it
- **Kill criteria** - formats underperforming for 4+ weeks get cut
- **Platform-native analytics** - Instagram Insights and TikTok Analytics as primary sources; third-party tools only for cross-posting dashboards

**Gate:** Weekly analytics ritual with retention review, top-decile log, and iteration backlog.

## Practitioner Grounding & Decision Rules

Low-confidence field (per master map §16): the best organic short-form operators are creators, not marketers, and most public claims are self-reported. This skill encodes format mechanics from creator teardowns and flags everything else as T3.

- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds** (creator consensus — HEURISTIC, T3): pattern interrupt or specific promise; the first frame decides distribution.
- **Retention loops beat polish** (creator consensus — HEURISTIC, T3): open loops, payoff structure, and rewatch value determine how far the algorithm pushes a video.
- **Volume + format repetition is the mechanism** (creator consensus — HEURISTIC, T3): consistent format repetition lets the algorithm learn the audience; format-hopping resets it.

Decision rules:
1. IF a video's first 1.5s doesn't interrupt THEN rewrite the hook before publishing — distribution is decided there (T3).
2. IF formats keep changing THEN commit to one format for 20+ posts before judging it (T3).
3. IF views grow but follows don't THEN the content is discovery-optimized but not identity-building — add a repeatable point of view (T3).
4. IF a claim about "growth hacks" lacks platform-native evidence THEN treat it as T3 and don't encode it (discipline rule).

## Metrics

- **Retention curve** (first-3s hold, mid-video drop) per format (T3).
- **Follow conversion** (follows ÷ views) — the identity metric (T3).

## Evaluation & QA

### Short-Form Health Rubric
| Criteria | Weak | OK | Strong |
|----------|------|----|--------|
| Hook strength | Idea starts at second 5 | Hook in first 3 seconds | First 2 seconds + pattern interrupt |
| Retention | Cliff at second 1 | Steady first half | Looped ending drives rewatches |
| Cadence | Sporadic | Held floor | Batch production, never missed |
| Formats | Random videos | 1-2 series | Series + comment-reply loop |
| Conversion | Views only | Profile visits tracked | Follower-to-email funnel measured |

### Common Failure Modes
- Obsessing over production quality while the hook is weak - retention dies before quality shows
- Posting everything everywhere - each platform needs its own packaging
- Chasing every trend - brand dilution and burnout
- Ignoring comments - the cheapest reach is the comment section you already have
- Judging by views alone - follower and email conversion is the growth that compounds
- Quitting before 30 videos - short-form is a volume game until a format finds its audience (heuristic, not a guarantee)
