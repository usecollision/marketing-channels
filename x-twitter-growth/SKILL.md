## When to Use

Invoke when:
- Building a founder or brand account from zero
- Impressions exist but followers do not convert
- Posts get no engagement
- Starting thread-based content
- Follower growth has stalled

## Workflow

### Step 1: Profile & Positioning Audit
- **Niche statement** - one sentence on who you serve and what you post about; every post should fit it
- **Bio** - value + proof + CTA in one readable block; update when the offer changes
- **Handle & name** - the name field carries searchable keywords; the handle stays brandable
- **Pinned post** - your best-performing proof or a self-introduction that converts profile visitors
- **Visuals** - banner and avatar consistent with the niche (see marketing-messaging/brand-voice)

**Gate:** Audit done; bio, pinned post, and niche statement rewritten and published.

### Step 2: Content Strategy & Format Mix
- **Format mix** - long-form threads, single insights, replies, visual posts, occasional media; a common working mix is a majority of insight posts plus a minority of personal/proof posts (heuristic - keep a few proof-of-work posts per week)
- **Thread structure** - hook in post 1, one idea per post, build to a payoff, CTA at the end; write the hook after the thread is drafted
- **Idea engine** - mine customer calls, questions you answer repeatedly, and contrarian takes (see marketing-intelligence/customer-research); keep a backlog of 30+ ideas
- **Repurposing** - long posts become threads; threads become carousels and LinkedIn posts (see linkedin-content)
- **Cadence** - consistency beats volume; a common heuristic is 1-3 posts per day including replies - pick what you can hold for 90 days

**Gate:** Content strategy with format mix, thread template, idea backlog, and cadence.

### Step 3: Algorithm & Format Mechanics
- **Early engagement** - the first hour matters for reach (widely observed pattern - treat as heuristic, verify on your account); post when your audience is active
- **Replies as distribution** - value-add replies on big accounts in your niche reach their audiences; budget time for this daily
- **Formatting** - line breaks between short sentences, no hashtag walls (heuristic - 1-3 hashtags max), one clear idea per post
- **Hooks** - lead with the strongest line; patterns - contrarian take, number-led list, mistake reveal, personal lesson
- **No engagement bait** - the platform penalizes follow-for-follow and bait patterns; grow with earned attention only
- **Community Notes** - fact-check claims before posting; a note on a viral post erases trust

**Gate:** Mechanics checklist applied to every post before publishing.

### Step 4: Engagement Tactics
- **Comment-first strategy** - leave genuine replies on 5-10 niche-relevant posts per day (heuristic); comments convert to profile visits
- **Reply to every comment** - early replies boost thread distribution and build the habit
- **Collaboration** - quote-post other creators with added value, join Spaces, co-post threads with adjacent accounts
- **DM etiquette** - follow up with engaged accounts personally; no mass auto-DMs
- **Consistency ritual** - fixed daily creation/engagement blocks; batch-write threads weekly

**Gate:** Daily engagement routine defined with time blocks and reply standards.

### Step 5: Audience Building & Conversion
- **Follower conversion path** - profile → pinned post → bio link/newsletter signup; measure profile-visit-to-follower rate
- **Lead magnet** - a downloadable artifact behind the bio link converts better than a homepage link (heuristic - offer something specific)
- **Launch moments** - time major threads to product launches and events (see product-launch-playbook)
- **Cross-pollination** - carry X moments to the newsletter and community, and vice versa (see newsletter-operations)

**Gate:** Conversion path mapped with links, magnet, and measurement.

### Step 6: Analytics & Iteration
- **Core metrics** - impressions, engagement rate, profile visits, follower conversion, link clicks (platform analytics or third-party dashboards)
- **Weekly review** - rank posts by engagement per 1000 impressions; double down on the top themes and formats
- **Kill criteria** - formats below your median for 4+ weeks get cut
- **Audience signals** - track which topics attract followers who convert, not just impressions

**Gate:** Weekly review cadence with a running top-performer log and format kill list.

## Evaluation & QA

### Growth Health Rubric
| Criteria | Weak | OK | Strong |
|----------|------|----|--------|
| Niche clarity | Posts about everything | Consistent topic | Sharp one-sentence niche |
| Consistency | Sporadic | Daily-ish | 90-day streak held |
| Hooks | Generic titles | Curiosity hooks | Hook bank with proven winners |
| Engagement | Post-and-leave | Replies to comments | Daily comment-first routine |
| Conversion | Follower count only | Bio link tracked | Follower-to-email conversion measured |

### Common Failure Modes
- Chasing impressions with off-niche viral posts that attract the wrong followers
- Threads with a weak first line - the rest is invisible
- Posting only about yourself - no value for the reader
- Ignoring replies - kills the thread and the relationship
- Buying followers or engagement - kills reach and credibility
- Quitting before 90 days - X compounding is slow until it is not (heuristic expectation, not a guarantee)
